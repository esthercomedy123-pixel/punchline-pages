# Comedy Spotlight

Absolutely — here’s a polished prompt you can paste directly into Lovable. I’ve made it specific enough to generate a strong first version while leaving your personal details as easy placeholders.

Build a modern, colorful, professional comedy website for me as a comedian and comedy coach.

The website should feel fun, energetic, bold, and contemporary, while still looking polished enough for comedy clubs, event organizers, and potential students. Use a vibrant color palette, playful typography, subtle animations, large visuals, and plenty of personality. The design should work beautifully on both desktop and mobile.

Website Structure

1. Hero / Home Section

Create a strong opening section that immediately tells visitors who I am and what I do.

Include:

My name: [YOUR NAME]

Headline: Comedian • Comedy Coach • Entertainer

A short, funny tagline: [YOUR TAGLINE]

A professional/funny photo area

Prominent buttons:

Book Me

Learn Comedy

See My Schedule

The hero should immediately communicate that this is a comedy website and have an energetic, entertaining feel.

2. About My Comedy

Create a short section introducing me and my style of comedy.

Include placeholders for:

My comedy style

My background

Where I perform

What makes my comedy unique

Keep the writing entertaining and conversational rather than sounding like a corporate biography.

3. "The Inflation Game" Show

Make this a major featured section of the website.

The show is called "The Inflation Game."

Give it its own bold, visually exciting section with:

Show title

Short description

Space for promotional photos

What audiences can expect

Upcoming show dates

A Get Tickets / Learn More button

A Book The Show button if appropriate

Make the visual design of this section distinctive from the rest of the site. Use playful visual references to money, prices, shopping, or inflation without making it look childish.

Include placeholder copy that I can easily edit later.

4. Comedy Coaching

Create a section promoting my comedy coaching lessons.

Title it something like "Learn the Craft of Comedy" or "Comedy Coaching."

Explain that I help aspiring and developing comedians improve their skills.

Include areas for:

Writing jokes

Stage presence

Delivery and timing

Finding your comedic voice

Performing in front of audiences

Developing material

Getting better at stand-up

Include a clear Book a Coaching Session button.

Create a simple pricing/lesson-card area with editable placeholders for:

Individual coaching

Coaching packages

Group lessons/workshops

Do not invent actual prices. Use placeholders such as "$___".

5. Schedule / Upcoming Shows

Create a clean, easy-to-update schedule section.

Display upcoming performances in attractive event cards containing:

Date

Time

Venue

City

Show name

Ticket/link button

Include a clear Book Me for Your Comedy Club call-to-action near the schedule.

Make it easy for me to add, remove, and update performances later.

6. Booking Section

Create a dedicated section for comedy clubs, venues, event planners, and other people who want to book me.

Headline:
"Want Me at Your Club?"

Explain that people can contact me about:

Comedy club performances

Private events

Corporate events

Festivals

Hosting/MC work

Special comedy shows

Include a prominent Book Me button that leads to a booking/contact form.

The booking form should collect:

Name

Email

Phone (optional)

Venue/Organization

Event date

Event location

Type of event

Additional information

Make the form simple and professional.

7. Contact Section

Create a dedicated contact section at the bottom of the website.

Include:

Email: [YOUR EMAIL]

Phone: [YOUR PHONE]

Social media links: [INSTAGRAM] [TIKTOK] [YOUTUBE] [FACEBOOK]

A contact form

Give visitors clear choices such as:
Book Me | Comedy Coaching | General Question

8. Footer

Create a colorful, memorable footer containing:

My name

Social media icons

Contact information

Quick navigation links

Copyright information

Add a small humorous line in the footer that fits my comedy brand.

Design Direction

The site should NOT look like a generic business template.

Make it feel like a real comedian's personal brand:

Bright, modern colors

Bold typography

Playful shapes

Fun animations and hover effects

High-quality image sections

Strong calls to action

Modern cards and buttons

Subtle comedy-related visual elements

Excellent mobile responsiveness

Use a sophisticated color palette rather than simply making everything rainbow-colored. Consider a bold combination such as electric blue, purple, yellow, orange, black, and white, with strong contrast.

Use large, expressive typography for section headings.

Add subtle animations when sections enter the screen, but keep the site fast and professional.

Functionality

Make the website fully responsive.

Navigation should include:
Home | About | The Inflation Game | Comedy Coaching | Schedule | Book Me | Contact

Make the navigation sticky on desktop and mobile-friendly.

All buttons and forms should work or have clearly marked placeholders for where integrations will be added.

Use reusable components so I can easily update:

Show dates

Venues

Photos

Social media links

Coaching information

Contact information

Biography

Do not make up personal information, show dates, venues, testimonials, prices, or achievements. Use clearly labeled placeholders wherever information is missing.

Overall, the finished website should make visitors think:

"This comedian is funny, professional, and someone I want to see perform or book."
